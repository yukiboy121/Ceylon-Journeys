About Ceylon Journeys Sri Lanka

Colombo-based tour operator helping locals and foreign visitors explore Sri Lanka since 2018.

Our Story

Your Sri Lanka Travel Partner

Ceylon Journeys started on Galle Road, Colombo with a simple idea – make it easy to book quality tours across Sri Lanka without confusing prices or unreliable drivers.

Today we arrange day trips, multi-day packages, and full island tours covering the Cultural Triangle, hill country, southern beaches, east coast, and wildlife parks like Yala and Udawalawe.
